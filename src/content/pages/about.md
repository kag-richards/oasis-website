## Our mission

Walk alongside women recovering from drug and alcohol addiction in a Christ-centered environment as they journey toward self sufficiency.

## Our vision

Create a safe haven where women in recovery can walk in freedom, and be empowered and equipped to live a life with dignity and purpose.

## What we believe

Every woman deserves access to the basics. Food. Shelter. Healthcare. A door that locks.

We believe that a woman leaving a recovery program is not a risk to be managed. She is someone who has already done the hardest thing, and who needs a bridge long enough to reach solid ground. Most programs end at discharge. The gap that opens up right after is where we work.

We serve women regardless of their ability to pay, and we do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin, disability, age, or any other characteristic protected by law.

## Our charitable purpose

Oasis Women Center is organized and operated exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

The organization provides transitional housing, transportation assistance, educational support, and mental health resources for women who have completed a drug or alcohol recovery program, to promote their successful reintegration into society and to help them achieve stability and self sufficiency.

## Who we serve

Women who have completed a drug or alcohol recovery program and who are homeless, at imminent risk of homelessness, or otherwise without safe and stable housing.

Residents generally are low income or very low income, at or below 30 percent of the Area Median Income as defined by HUD, or can otherwise demonstrate financial hardship. Priority goes to women in immediate danger, women who are literally homeless, and women with the fewest alternatives.

**Selection is never based on ability to pay.**

## Where we are

We serve Corinth and the surrounding Denton County area.

Our transitional housing location is kept confidential. This is standard practice for residential programs serving women in crisis, and it protects the safety and privacy of our residents. For anything you need, our phone number and email are on the [contact page](/contact/).

## Our standing

Oasis Women Center was founded in 2024 and is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, EIN 99-1931849, incorporated in the State of Texas. Contributions are tax deductible to the extent allowed by law.
