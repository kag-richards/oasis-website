## Why our street address is not listed

Oasis Women Center is a residential program serving women recovering from addiction, many of whom are leaving unsafe situations. We keep our housing location confidential to protect their safety and privacy. This is standard practice for programs like ours.

If you need our mailing address for a donation, a legal notice, or grant paperwork, email or call us and we will provide it directly.
